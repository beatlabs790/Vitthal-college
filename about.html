About Our College

Discover the legacy, vision, and driving force behind Vitthal College of Education.

Legacy of Excellence
Since 2013

Established under the Manaviya Vibhava Vikas Nyas (registered in 1986), the Vitthal College of Education has been a beacon of teacher training since its inception in 2013.

We mark ourselves as a 'panache' among colleges in Bihar. Our core motto is to prepare quality teachers who provide education grounded in good morals, self-reliance, equality, and social justice. We are a co-educational institution that takes pride in women empowerment, providing girls with extensive exposure for holistic personality development.

Through pedagogy consisting of context, experience, reflection, action, and evaluation, we aim for the integral training of young men and women.

Our Vision

To develop the students and trainees by way of various standard norms and impart training through modern, updated ways to get suitable employment aiming for justice, freedom, and harmony for the growth of skills at the school and college level.

Our Mission

To impart training to educationally motivated students who will become professionally competent with commitment towards building a society inspired spiritually with high moral values, resulting in social transformation.

Guiding the Future

"Education is not merely the accumulation of facts, but the preparation of life itself. At Vitthal College of Education, our primary objective is to nurture aspiring educators who possess not just academic brilliance, but deep-rooted empathy, unwavering ethics, and the resilience to shape the next generation of our nation. We strive every day to create an environment where enjoyable learning meets strict punctuality and social equality."

Aims & Objectives

Core principles that guide our academic curriculum and institutional culture.

Subject Mastery

Deepen and improve teaching skills to develop capacity and comprehensive mastery in respective academic subjects.

Individual Rights

Fostering a deep respect for the fundamental rights of every individual within a democratic society.

National Loyalty

Preparing and motivating students to take active leadership roles for the holistic betterment of their country.

Enjoyable Learning

Making the teaching-learning process an enjoyable, interactive, and highly creative experience for all.

Punctuality & Discipline

Developing the strict discipline to plan and complete educational work within stipulated time frames.

Social Equality

Cultivating a strong feeling of equality, secularism, and unwavering moral uprightness among trainees.
