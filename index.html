Welcome to Vitthal College

The Vitthal College of Education (B.Ed. College) was established under the Manaviya Vibhava Vikas Nyas society in the year 2013. The M.V.V Nyas, registered since 1986, has been dedicated to the objective of providing high-quality teacher training.

The institution is committed to achieving higher standards of integrated and personalized training through advanced technology and practical know-how. We aim to bestow all positive efforts to the learners to become good teachers and citizens.
