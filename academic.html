Eligibility & Entrance

Admission is granted through the **Combined Entrance Test (CET)** as per University directions. Candidates must have at least **50% marks** in Bachelor's or Master's degree.

Required Documents

  • CET Qualified Score Card
  • Matric Marksheet & Certificate
  • Intermediate (+2) Marksheet
  • Degree Marksheet / CLC
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • 3 Passport Size Photos

Rules & Regulations

80%

Minimum attendance required for theory and practical classes as per University norms.

•

Prior permission from the Principal is mandatory for any absence.

•

Name will be dropped from rolls if absent continuously for more than 10 days without sanction.

Note: English and Hindi are both held as mediums of instruction. Examinations can be written in either language.
